Hi,

Here's a summary of the support agent before we talk about going live.


What it does

The agent handles the most common support requests on its own:
- It asks every customer for their email and last 4 card digits before doing
  anything. If they don't match what's on file, it shares nothing and takes no
  action - no exceptions.
- It issues refunds automatically when the transaction is settled, under 30 days
  old, $75 or less, and hasn't already been refunded.
- For everything else it can't handle, it creates a ticket and hands the case to
  your team with a short summary of the conversation.

What it won't do

- It won't refund anything over $75 or older than 30 days, those go straight to
  your team.
- It won't unfreeze a card, and it won't guess why a card was frozen. It tells the
  customer the card is frozen and escalates.
- It won't act on anything involving legal threats, fraud claims over $500, or
  account closure, those go to a human immediately.
- It won't promise anything the policy doesn't cover.

One important note on safety: even if the AI model makes a mistake and tries to
issue a refund it shouldn't, a hard code-level check will block it. Refunds that shouldn't happen
won't happen regardless of what the model says.

What I need from you before launch

I made a few calls where the brief didn't give me a clear answer. I went
conservative, but you should confirm these before we go live:

1. Frozen card + eligible refund: right now any action on a frozen card goes to
   your team. If a customer with a frozen card has a refund that would otherwise
   qualify, should it still go through, or should frozen always mean escalate?

2. Pending transactions: the agent won't refund a charge that hasn't settled yet.
   Is that the right call, or should it handle pending differently?

3. Fraud under $500: the brief says fraud over $500 goes to a human. I treat all
   fraud claims as escalations regardless of amount. Should smaller fraud claims
   follow the normal refund rules instead?

4. Freeze reason: the brief mentions the agent can explain why a card is frozen,
   but the API doesn't return a reason, just that the card is frozen. If you want
   the agent to communicate the reason, that field needs to be added to the API.

5. The $75 and 30-day boundaries: I treated $75 as inclusive (a $75 charge
   qualifies) and 30 days as exclusive (a charge from exactly 30 days ago does
   not). Please confirm that's right.
